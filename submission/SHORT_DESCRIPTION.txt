TESSERA is a governance-first wealth decision-intelligence layer for Relationship Managers. Instead of ranking noisy market alerts, it detects where a client’s intent, bank-held portfolio, future obligations, and policy constraints disagree. It replays five portfolio snapshots against an authoritative event log, looks through structured products, rehearses transparent counterfactuals, and turns each finding into an evidence-backed client conversation the RM can approve, edit, or reject.
