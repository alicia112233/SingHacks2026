# Team photo required before submission

No real team photograph or team-member information was present in the repository. The original pitch deck contains an editable photo placeholder; the improved v2 deck uses the same panel for a pilot scorecard rather than fabricating a group image.

Before hand-in:

1. Replace the right-side panel on the final slide with a real landscape group photo if the photo must appear inside the submitted deck.
2. Add team name and member names.
3. Use PowerPoint **Picture Format → Compress Pictures → HD (330 ppi)**.

Do not submit the placeholder as the mandatory team picture.
