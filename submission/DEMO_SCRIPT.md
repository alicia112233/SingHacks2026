# TESSERA — three-minute presentation + demo

The timed presentation ends on slide 6. Slides A1–A3 are Q&A backups only.

## 0:00–0:15 — Slide 1: The portfolio report is not the story

“Twenty clients, one RM, and far more data than one person can review before markets open. A portfolio report can be accurate and still miss the conversation that matters. TESSERA finds that conversation.”

## 0:15–0:40 — Slide 2: Our USP

“We do not rank yesterday’s market noise. We rank contradictions between what the client wants and what the evidence says. These are three deliberately deep decision rooms—not the numerical top three. Lau proves concentration plus collateral risk, Abdullah proves look-through and outside-business correlation, and Cheung proves honest handling of conflicting evidence.”

## 0:40–1:00 — Slide 3: The RM-controlled loop

“TESSERA turns fragmented records into four questions: what happened, what could happen, what should we consider, and what will the RM say? Suitability and data controls run before wording. Every answer carries an evidence passport, and Priscilla remains responsible for the advice.”

## 1:00–1:25 — Slide 4: Why Abdullah

“Abdullah asked to diversify away from Gulf shipping. Yet 42.1% of his bank-held portfolio is linked to shipping and energy after looking through the FCN—and his operating business depends on the same conditions. He needs USD 5 million for a Singapore family office, versus USD 2.4 million in cash. His recent gains make this contradiction harder to see.”

## 1:25–2:30 — Live demo

1. Open Abdullah’s decision room from the review queue.
2. Show **Client says / Portfolio does / Upcoming constraint**.
3. Toggle **Strait reopens** and **Blockade worsens**; say these are transparent sensitivities, not forecasts.
4. Open the evidence passport to show the FCN look-through and source records.
5. Finish on the RM action: ring-fence the seed capital and map the USD 2.6 million cash gap.

If the live demo fails, remain on slide 4 and explain the two scenario bars; then move directly to slide 5.

## 2:30–2:45 — Slide 5: Work done

“This is not a slide mock-up. The prototype joins eleven datasets, ranks the full client book, opens a review room for every client, recalculates scenarios, and logs RM decisions. All 18 Python tests pass. In production, the adapters change; the evidence, control, and decision workflow remains.”

## 2:45–3:00 — Slide 6: Close

“TESSERA makes the portfolio defensible, the future discussable, and the RM indispensable. Our ask is a shadow-mode pilot with real RM decisions: prove decision quality first, then earn the right to generate.”

Stop and invite questions.

## Two-minute Q&A backups

- **A1 — Why Lau?** Four wrappers create 49.0% property exposure; HKD 60 million is due; the real trigger cushion is HKD 0.71 million.
- **A2 — Why Cheung?** Conflicting draw records lower confidence and become the first client question; the system does not fabricate a convenient answer.
- **A3 — Can this operate inside a bank?** Read-only records, temporal joins, policy before language, purpose-limited private deployment, and an RM-controlled decision ledger.
