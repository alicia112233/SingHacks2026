# TESSERA — three-minute demo

**0:00–0:30 — Today**

Open the decision queue. Explain that TESSERA ranks client contradictions rather than volatility. Every score exposes its inputs and the top band is deliberately limited to five conversations, matching one RM's capacity.

**0:30–1:20 — Lau Chi Ming**

Open Lau's decision room. Walk left to right through “client says / portfolio does / future demands.” Show the 49.0% look-through Hong Kong property exposure. Point out that 69.41% LTV is only 0.59 percentage point below the trigger and that the real trigger buffer is HKD 0.71m—not the much larger reported headroom field. Run the property stress.

**1:20–2:05 — Abdullah Al-Mansoori**

Switch to Abdullah. Show that the successful shipping-and-energy thesis has recreated the operating-business risk the portfolio was meant to diversify. Toggle between “Strait reopens” and “Blockade worsens”; emphasise transparent assumptions and no assigned probability.

**2:05–2:40 — Cheung Kwok Wing**

Show the retirement-income tension: 66.6% fixed income, one Treasury due 2045, and cash needs. Open the evidence passport and show the USD 1.10m / USD 1.28m contradiction. TESSERA asks the RM to verify rather than letting the model choose a convenient number.

**2:40–3:00 — Evidence ledger**

Close on the governance architecture: event authority, suitability-before-language, purpose-limited evidence bundles, private deployment, and RM approval.
