Since SingHacks · shifts 1 to 3

From a prototype to a tool a bank can run

1

Client data stays in the bank

Outside models read public headlines only. Actions are reviewed by the bank’s own model.

2

Screening, not predicting

A headline flags the clients it touches and what to check. No forecasts.

3

Detection for any client

Code compares each client’s own words with their holdings. No client is named in the rules.

Shaped by our check with a relationship manager and our own research.
