Why RMs can trust it

Built for how a private bank works

Client data stays in the bank

Outside models only read public headlines. Client data goes only to the bank’s own model.

Every number has a source

Each figure traces to a record, and says if it is client data, a calculation, an illustration or news.

AI can only lower confidence

The review takes the lower of the code’s score and the model’s. No forecasts; scenarios are labelled.

The RM decides, on the record

Approve, edit or dismiss. Every decision goes into an append-only ledger.

[Teammate] · 30 seconds. “Why can an RM trust it? Client data never leaves the bank: outside models only read public headlines. Every number traces to a source and says what kind of number it is. The AI can only lower confidence, never raise it, and nothing is a forecast. And the RM decides: every approval, edit or dismissal is on the record.”
