Book map · customer vectors

Every client is a vector. Lookalike books sit together.

TESSERA describes each client with the same named numbers: exposures after looking through funds and notes, how easily the portfolio turns to cash, what the client owes, and what they told us. Comparing those numbers shows which books look alike, and how far each one has drifted from what the client asked for. Tap a client.

How it works

Named numbers, calculated inside the bank

Each client gets the same list of dimensions, calculated by code from data the bank already holds. Each dimension is ranked across the book, so one very large position cannot drown out everything else, and each group carries equal weight.

Similarity is the cosine between two clients' vectors. Every match comes with its reasons: the dimensions where both clients sit above the book average.

Why not an AI embedding? An embedding model would need client data sent to it, and its dimensions cannot be explained. Named numbers stay inside the bank, and an RM can always see why two clients look alike. This is a similarity measure, not a prediction.
